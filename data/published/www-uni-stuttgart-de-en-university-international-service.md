## Changes to our regular office hours

**Summer break**

From **Monday, August 3 to Friday, August 14, 2026** there will be no open office hours at the International Office. During this period, many staff members will not be available or have only limited access to their e-mails. The German Courses will take place as usual.

The **first open office hours** after this short summer break will take place on **Monday, August 17, 2 – 4 p.m.**

## Office hours

The International Office offers **on-site** open office hours to **international students** and to all students interested in **studying abroad:**

- March-April & September-October: Monday & Wednesday 2 p.m. to 4 p.m., Tuesday & Thursday 10 a.m. to noon
- In the remaining period: Monday & Wednesday 2 p.m. to 4 p.m.

Some of our staff members still work from home. If you want to talk to a specific person, we recommend you make an appointment.

**International researchers** and **doctoral students** should make an appointment for a personal consultation.

Come and see us at the IZ!

**Online** office hours at the International Office are only offered to students interested in **studying abroad**:

- Monday, 10 a.m. to 12 p.m.

**International researchers** and **doctoral students** should make an appoinment for a personal online consultation.
