# Apply for a residence permit - for the purpose of study, language course, school attendance

If you come from a country outside the EU or the EEA (Norway, Iceland or Liechtenstein) and would like to study or attend a school or language course in Germany, you usually need a residence permit. The residence permit is temporary and can be extended upon application.
