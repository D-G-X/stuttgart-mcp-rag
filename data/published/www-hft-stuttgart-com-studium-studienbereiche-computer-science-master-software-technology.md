# Master Software Technology

The Master’s program in Software Technology is designed for graduates with a bachelor’s degree in computer science or a related field who wish to deepen their knowledge of software development and application in a targeted manner.


The goal of the program is to train professionals for demanding development and leadership roles in an international environment. Based on a scientific foundation, the program teaches current methods, technologies, and standards in software engineering that are necessary for the development and operation of complex software systems. In addition, key skills such as teamwork, communication, and project-oriented work are fostered.


The three-semester, internationally oriented program is closely aligned with industry requirements. Companies are actively involved in the curriculum and contribute practical perspectives. Students work with modern technologies in fields such as software engineering, information management, artificial intelligence, and e-commerce.


All courses are conducted in English.

Applicants to the master’s program must:

- Hold an above-average degree in computer science or a related field from a German university or a comparable foreign institution, with a minimum of 7 semesters and 210 credit points (ECTS).
- Demonstrable competencies from the first degree in the following areas
- Programming (knowledge of at least one object-oriented programming language)

1. Software engineering
2. Database systems
3. Data structures and algorithms
4. Distributed systems

- Proficiency in English, typically demonstrated by an IELTS score of 6.5 or higher or a TOEFL score (computer-based: at least 213 points; paper-based: at least 550 points; internet-based: at least 85 points)

The first and second semester covers advanced theories, methods and techniques in the field of software development, data management, project management and computer networking. Amongst others this comprises the lectures "Advanced Topics in Software Engineering", "System Design", "Advanced Topics in Databases", "Middleware Technology" and "Advanced Topcis in Software Project Management". An elective module each semester allows deepening the knowledge in formal methods (e.g. "Cryptographic Algorithms") and in newest technological issues (e.g. "Business Process Technology"). A large software-project – spanning the whole 2nd semester – gives practical foundation to the theories and methods above.
