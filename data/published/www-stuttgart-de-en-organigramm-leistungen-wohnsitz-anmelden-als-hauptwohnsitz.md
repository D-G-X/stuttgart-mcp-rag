# Register residence - as main residence

If you move to Stuttgart, you must register at a **Citizens’ Service Center** within **two weeks** of moving in. You can also use our **online service**.

If you register your residence in person at one of the 22 citizen service offices in the city, you can take care of other services related to your move at the same time. For example:

- Updating your address on the chip of your ID card
- Receiving an address sticker for your ID card
- Updating the address on your passport or eAT
- Transferring your vehicle registration to your new address
- Obtaining resident parking permits for your new residence

Please check directly at a citizen service office to see if you are exempt from the registration requirement. For example, you may be exempt from the registration requirement if you are registered in Germany and are living in Stuttgart for a maximum of six months for work or educational reasons. This also applies, for example, if you are moving to Stuttgart from abroad and will only be living here for a maximum of three months.

**Note:**

You will need a landlord’s certificate from your landlord. This can be done online.

## Online service

## Notes on the online service

You enter your data on the platform of the service portal of the state of Baden-Württemberg www.service-bw.de (opens in a new tab).

As a rule, you must set up a personal service account on the service portal to submit your application. This allows you to start, edit, temporarily save, and also submit your application. The Ministry of the Interior of Baden-Württemberg is responsible for processing your data in the service account.

## Procedure

You have the option to fill out the forms in advance. You can download them here and then submit them in person at the Citizens’ Office. You may also register your family members if they have been living with you in the same residence and are moving with you. To do so, you must bring their identification documents. If your children do not have identification documents, please bring their birth certificates. Families can be registered on a single registration form.

Authorized representatives must also present a power of attorney and their own identification.

After registration, you will receive a registration confirmation for your records.

With the **electronic residence registration,** you can easily **notify** the registration office **of** your new address **online.** You will receive a digital registration confirmation. You can also update your ID card, passport, or eID card via the online service. The easiest way to do this is with your smartphone. You can find more information here:  www.wohnsitzanmeldung.de (opens in a new tab)

 

**Note:** When moving out of your old residence, you no longer need to deregister; this happens automatically.

## Required documents

- Identification card or passport
- Identification documents for family members: For children who do not have identification documents, you must provide a birth certificate
- Certificate from the landlord: Landlord’s confirmation PDF-File 605,61 kB
- Electronic residence permit for all persons, if available
- When registering with a power of attorney: Power of attorney to be submitted to the Citizens’ Office PDF-File 491,60 kB, copies of ID card or passport

**Note**: Your landlord is required to confirm your move into the new residence in writing. Submit the  landlord’s confirmation PDF-File
 605,61 kB to the Citizens’ Office. If you are moving into your own property, you must complete the confirmation yourself. Your landlord can also provide the confirmation online:  Landlord’s confirmation.**Tip:** While you’re at it, it’s best to have the address on your ID card, passport, and Vehicle Registration Certificate I (vehicle registration) updated as well.

## Forms

## Deadlines

You must register within two weeks of moving in.

## Processing time

Your identification documents will be changed immediately on the spot in a citizens' office. You will receive a registration confirmation for your records.
