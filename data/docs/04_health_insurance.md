# Health Insurance (Krankenversicherung)

Health insurance is legally mandatory in Germany. You cannot enroll at a university or, in most cases, be granted a student visa/residence permit without proof of valid coverage.

## Two systems
1. **Public health insurance (Gesetzliche Krankenversicherung, GKV)**
   - Available to students generally **under 30** who are in a regular degree program.
   - Cost is roughly **€120–€130/month** (2025/2026 range; check current rates with a provider like TK, AOK, or Barmer).
2. **Private health insurance (Private Krankenversicherung, PKV)**
   - Required for students **over 30**, PhD students, guest researchers, and those in non-regular degree programs (e.g. some preparatory/language courses), since these groups are generally not eligible for the public student rate.

## EU vs. non-EU students
- EU/EEA students can often rely on their European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) initially, but may still need to register with a German provider for longer stays.
- Non-EU students generally need to take out German coverage (public or private) or an insurer explicitly recognized by German authorities as equivalent.

## Practical notes
- Get your insurance confirmation **before** enrollment — the university and the Ausländerbehörde both require it.
- If starting with private insurance short-term (e.g. travel/incoming insurance before arrival), plan the switch to public/long-term coverage promptly after arrival to avoid enrollment issues.
