# Registering Your Address (Anmeldung) in Stuttgart

Anyone moving into a home in Germany must register their address ("Wohnsitz anmelden") with the local citizens' office (Bürgerbüro) within **two weeks** of moving in. This applies to international students as well, and the confirmation you receive (Meldebescheinigung) is required for almost every later step: opening a bank account, applying for or extending a residence permit, and enrolling at the university.

## What you need
- Valid passport/ID
- Landlord's confirmation of move-in (**Wohnungsgeberbestätigung**) — your landlord or the housing office must provide this
- Completed registration form (available at the Bürgerbüro or online)

## How to do it
1. Book an appointment online via Stuttgart's Bürgerbüro appointment system.
2. Attend in person at one of Stuttgart's citizens' offices (see office locations doc).
3. Receive your **Meldebescheinigung** (registration confirmation) on the spot — keep several copies, you'll need them repeatedly.

## Notes
- An "electronic residence registration" option also exists for some cases via wohnsitzanmeldung.de, but in-person registration at a Bürgerbüro is the standard route for new arrivals.
- Appointments can be booked weeks in advance during peak season (semester start) — book as early as possible.
