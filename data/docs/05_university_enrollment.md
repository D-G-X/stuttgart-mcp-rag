# Enrollment (Immatriculation) at the University of Stuttgart

Once admitted, international students must formally enroll ("immatrikulieren") to become an official student. Enrollment at the University of Stuttgart is handled online via the campus management system **C@MPUS**.

## Typical requirements
- Letter of admission
- Proof of health insurance (see health insurance doc)
- Proof of address registration (Meldebescheinigung, see Anmeldung doc) — sometimes accepted shortly after enrollment if not yet available
- Passport photo, valid ID/passport
- For applicants without a German high school diploma: proof of German language proficiency (e.g. DSH/TestDaF), unless the program is taught fully in English and exempts this

## International Office (Dezernat Internationales)
The International Office (IO) supports international students and doctoral students with formalities, financial/social living questions, and general study questions.

**Address:** Pfaffenwaldring 60, 70569 Stuttgart (Building "IZ", Vaihingen campus)

**On-site open office hours (subject to change by semester):**
- March–April & September–October: Mon & Wed 14:00–16:00, Tue & Thu 10:00–12:00
- Remaining periods: Mon & Wed 14:00–16:00

*(Always check the current semester's hours on the University's website before visiting.)*
