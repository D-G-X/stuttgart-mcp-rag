# M.Sc. Software Technology at HFT Stuttgart

HFT Stuttgart (Hochschule für Technik Stuttgart, Stuttgart University of Applied Sciences) offers a Master of Science in Software Technology — a separate institution from the University of Stuttgart, with its own admissions and campus. All courses are taught in English.

## Program facts
- Degree: Master of Science (M.Sc.)
- Duration: 3 semesters full-time, or 5 semesters part-time (part-time open to EU students)
- Focus: advanced software engineering, information management, computer networks, and e-commerce, with a large software project spanning the second semester and a thesis (usually industry-based) in the third

## Entry requirements
- An above-average bachelor's degree in computer science or a related field, from a German university or a comparable foreign institution, with at least 7 semesters and 210 ECTS credit points
- Demonstrable competency from the first degree in: programming (at least one object-oriented language), software engineering, database systems, data structures and algorithms, and distributed systems
- English proficiency: IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL (paper-based 550+, computer-based 213+, internet-based 85+) — required for all applicants except native speakers

## Application
- Applications are submitted electronically via the online application portal
- Required documents: one-page CV/resume in English, a 500-word "Statement of Goals and Objectives," TOEFL/IELTS score report, secondary school leaving certificate, all higher-education degree transcripts (original language + English or German translation), 2 recommendation letters
- Applicants from India must additionally submit an APS certificate if available
- **Deadlines** — Winter semester (starts Oct 1): regular deadline Feb 28, additional deadline (if places remain) July 15. Summer semester (starts mid-March): regular deadline Aug 31, additional deadline (if places remain) Jan 15.

## Fees
- Enrollment fee: €192.50 for the first semester, €180.50 for each subsequent semester (as of June 2024 — verify current amount before applying)
- Additional non-EU international student fee: €1,500 per semester (Baden-Württemberg state fee, in effect since Winter 2017/18)

*(Fees and deadlines change — always verify the current figures on the official program page before applying.)*
