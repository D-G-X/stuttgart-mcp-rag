# Office Locations for Stuttgart International Student Bureaucracy

## Bürgerbüro (Citizens' Office) — for Anmeldung (address registration)
Stuttgart has several district citizens' offices; register at whichever covers your neighborhood, or the most convenient one accepting appointments. Locations include Bad Cannstatt, Süd, Mitte, Vaihingen, Mühlhausen, and Weilimdorf.

**Closest to the university (Vaihingen campus):**
Bürgerbüro Vaihingen
Rathausplatz 1, 70563 Stuttgart
Phone: 0711-21693700 · Email: buergerbueros@stuttgart.de

Opening hours:
- Mon: 08:30–13:00
- Tue: 08:30–13:00, 14:00–16:00
- Wed: 08:30–13:00 (mornings only, appointment required)
- Thu: 08:30–13:00, 14:00–18:00
- Fri: 08:30–13:00

*Note: Mondays and Thursday afternoons tend to be busy. Book an appointment online in advance via Stuttgart's Bürgerbüro appointment system.*
