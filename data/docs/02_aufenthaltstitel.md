# Residence Permit (Aufenthaltstitel) and Visa Extension for Students

Non-EU students who enter Germany on a national (D) student visa must convert it into a residence permit for study purposes ("Aufenthaltserlaubnis zum Zweck des Studiums") shortly after arrival, and extend it periodically until they graduate. In Stuttgart this is handled by the **Ausländerbehörde** (immigration office), part of the Amt für öffentliche Ordnung.

## Key facts
- The permit is issued as an **eAT** (elektronischer Aufenthaltstitel) — a credit-card-format ID with an embedded chip.
- Apply for an extension **1–2 months before your current permit expires**.
- Required documents typically include: current proof of enrollment (Immatrikulationsbescheinigung), current proof of funding (blocked account statement or equivalent), valid passport, biometric photo, and proof of health insurance.
- Applications in Stuttgart are submitted via the city's online portal — select "Residence permit for training" then "I would like to have my residence permit extended for the purpose of training."
- Processing times can be long; due to high application volume, it's common to receive a decision only shortly before the previous permit expires. Apply as early as the portal allows.
